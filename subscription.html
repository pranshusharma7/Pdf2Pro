FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is the 7-day Pro trial actually free?
Yes. No credit card required. You get full Pro access for 7 days. At the end, you choose to subscribe or go back to Free. We won't auto-charge you.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Absolutely. Cancel from your account settings with one click. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
Are my files stored permanently?
No. All uploaded and converted files are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours. We do not store or read your content.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), net banking, and PayPal. Payments are processed securely via Razorpay.
What's the difference between Pro and Business?
Pro is designed for individual power users — unlimited conversions, no watermarks, OCR, and cloud integrations. Business adds API access, team management for up to 10 users, custom webhooks, and 24/7 live chat support.
Is there a student or nonprofit discount?
Yes! Students and verified nonprofits get 40% off Pro. Email us at support@pdfpro.app with proof of eligibility and we'll send you a discount code within 24 hours.
